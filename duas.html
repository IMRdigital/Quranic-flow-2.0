🤲 50 Daily Duas

1. Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللهِ

In the name of Allah.

2. After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

All praise is for Allah.

3. Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

4. Upon Waking Up

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا

All praise is for Allah who gave us life.

5. Entering Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا

In the name of Allah we enter.

6. Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ

I place my trust in Allah.

7. Entering Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.

8. Leaving Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ

O Allah, I ask You for Your bounty.

9. Before Studying

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

10. Seeking Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ

I seek forgiveness from Allah.
