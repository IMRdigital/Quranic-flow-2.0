🤲 50 Daily Duas

1. Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللهِ

In the name of Allah.

2. After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

All praise is for Allah.

3. Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live.

4. Upon Waking Up

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا

All praise is for Allah who gave us life.

5. Entering Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا

In the name of Allah we enter.

6. Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللهِ

I place my trust in Allah.

7. Entering Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.

8. Leaving Mosque

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ

O Allah, I ask You for Your bounty.

9. Before Studying

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

10. Seeking Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ

I seek forgiveness from Allah.

11. For Parents

رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا

My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up when I was small.

12. For Guidance

اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ

Guide us to the Straight Path.

13. For Good in This World and Hereafter

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً

Our Lord, give us good in this world and the Hereafter.

14. Entering Bathroom

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْخُبُثِ وَالْخَبَائِثِ

O Allah, I seek refuge in You from evil.

15. Leaving Bathroom

غُفْرَانَكَ

I seek Your forgiveness.

16. Wearing New Clothes

اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ الْحَمْدُ

O Allah, all praise belongs to You.

17. Looking in Mirror

اللَّهُمَّ كَمَا حَسَّنْتَ خَلْقِي فَحَسِّنْ خُلُقِي

O Allah, beautify my character as You beautified my appearance.

18. For Anxiety

حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

Allah is sufficient for us and the best disposer of affairs.

19. Before Journey

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا

Glory be to Him who has subjected this to us.

20. Returning from Journey

آيِبُونَ تَائِبُونَ عَابِدُونَ

Returning, repenting, worshipping our Lord.

21. For Patience

رَبَّنَا أَفْرِغْ عَلَيْنَا صَبْرًا

Our Lord, pour upon us patience.

22. For Protection

أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ

I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah.

23. For Rizq

اللَّهُمَّ اكْفِنِي بِحَلَالِكَ عَنْ حَرَامِكَ

O Allah, suffice me with what You made lawful.

24. For Success

رَبِّ اشْرَحْ لِي صَدْرِي

My Lord, expand for me my chest.

25. For Ease

اللَّهُمَّ لَا سَهْلَ إِلَّا مَا جَعَلْتَهُ سَهْلًا

O Allah, there is no ease except what You make easy.

26. For Knowledge

رَّبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

My Lord, increase me in knowledge.

27. For Gratitude

رَبِّ أَوْزِعْنِي أَنْ أَشْكُرَ نِعْمَتَكَ

My Lord, enable me to be grateful for Your favors.

28. For Good Health

اللَّهُمَّ عَافِنِي فِي بَدَنِي

O Allah, grant me health in my body.

29. For Mercy

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ

My Lord, forgive and have mercy.

30. For Strength

لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ

There is no power nor strength except through Allah.

31. Entering Market

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ

None has the right to be worshipped except Allah alone.

32. During Rain

اللَّهُمَّ صَيِّبًا نَافِعًا

O Allah, make it beneficial rain.

33. After Rain

مُطِرْنَا بِفَضْلِ اللَّهِ وَرَحْمَتِهِ

We have been given rain by Allah's grace and mercy.

34. When Sneezing

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

All praise is due to Allah.

35. Reply to Sneezing

يَرْحَمُكَ اللَّهُ

May Allah have mercy on you.

36. For Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

I seek Allah's forgiveness and repent to Him.

37. For Contentment

رَضِيتُ بِاللَّهِ رَبًّا

I am pleased with Allah as my Lord.

38. For Trust in Allah

تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ

I place my trust in Allah.

39. For Paradise

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ الْجَنَّةَ

O Allah, I ask You for Paradise.

40. Protection from Hellfire

اللَّهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي مِنَ النَّارِ

O Allah, protect me from the Fire.

41. Morning Dua

أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ

We have entered the morning and all sovereignty belongs to Allah.

42. Evening Dua

أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ

We have entered the evening and all sovereignty belongs to Allah.

43. For Family

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ

Our Lord, grant us comfort in our spouses and children.

44. For Protection of Children

أُعِيذُكُمَا بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّةِ

I seek protection for you in the perfect words of Allah.

45. For Guidance of Heart

اللَّهُمَّ اهْدِ قَلْبِي

O Allah, guide my heart.

46. For Light

اللَّهُمَّ اجْعَلْ فِي قَلْبِي نُورًا

O Allah, place light in my heart.

47. For Ease of Affairs

وَيَسِّرْ لِي أَمْرِي

And make my task easy for me.

48. For Acceptance

رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا

Our Lord, accept from us.

49. For Steadfastness

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا

Our Lord, do not let our hearts deviate.

50. Final Comprehensive Dua

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً

Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter.
