📚 Hadith Collection

1. Importance of Intentions

Actions are judged by intentions, and every person will get the reward according to what he has intended.

Sahih al-Bukhari

2. Kindness

The best among you are those who have the best manners and character.

Sahih al-Bukhari

3. Smiling

Your smile for your brother is charity.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi

4. Seeking Knowledge

Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim.

Sunan Ibn Majah

5. Mercy

Whoever does not show mercy will not be shown mercy.

Sahih Muslim

6. Truthfulness

Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise.

Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim

7. Helping Others

Allah helps His servant as long as he helps his brother.

Sahih Muslim

8. Gratitude

He who does not thank people does not thank Allah.

Jami' at-Tirmidhi

9. Good Speech

Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day should speak good or remain silent.

Sahih al-Bukhari

10. Brotherhood

None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself.

Sahih al-Bukhari & Sahih Muslim
